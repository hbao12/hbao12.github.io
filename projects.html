Blue Jays Attendance Analytics

A comprehensive dataset spanning 2015–2025 of game-level Blue Jays data sourced from the MLB StatsAPI, including attendance figures, opponent profiles, stadium capacity, promotional schedules, and day-of-the-week effects. Projects built on this dataset include: forecasting 2026 game-by-game attendance using XGBoost regression; a Difference-in-Differences causal analysis of "Loonie Dogs" promotion nights; an A/B testing study of bobblehead giveaway impact; and a K-Means clustering segmentation of game-day dynamics for ticketing strategy optimization.

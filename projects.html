Do 'Loonie Dogs' Nights Actually Drive Attendance? A Difference-in-Differences Causal Analysis

This study applies a Difference-in-Differences (DiD) framework to estimate the true causal impact of "Loonie Dogs" promotions on game-day attendance for the Blue Jays. By comparing attendance trends on promotion days (the treatment group) against a comparable control group of non-promotional games, this analysis isolates the structural lift in ticket sales from confounding variables like seasonal demand and day-of-the-week effects.

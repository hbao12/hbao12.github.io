Postgres Python Dashboard

Canadian Salary Dashboard

Report analyzing Canadian job posting salaries. It queries a PostgreSQL database for postings with pay data, then visualizes median compensation across role families, seniority levels, industries, and canonical roles. The dashboard includes KPI cards (market median, pay coverage, management premium), auto-generated takeaways, bar/line charts, a role × seniority benchmark matrix, and detailed statistical tables. Output is uploaded to S3.

ATS Tracking Analytics

ATS Dashboard

Cross-tab analysis of applicant tracking systems, industry, and salary data coverage across 47,257 Canadian job postings. Reveals which ATS platforms dominate the market (Workday leads with 19,924 postings), how salary transparency varies by system (66.1% coverage on Workday vs. 32.1% overall), and industry-level patterns in compensation disclosure. Built by joining job postings to company ATS and industry metadata.
